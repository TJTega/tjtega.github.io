Space Launch VR

Led the programming team for an educational Virtual Reality experience developed by Falmouth University for Spaceport Cornwall, simulating the historic first satellite launch from UK soil. Built for deployment across UK schools and public VR stores, the interactive experience guides users from the Boeing 747 Cosmic Girl takeoff at Newquay Airport to rocket deployment at 35,000 feet and low Earth orbit, accompanied by narrations from leading scientists and astronauts.

A co-development project between Hungry Billy and Energym, creating an app that displays and communicates information back and forth from 16 of Energym’s energy generation exercise bikes, built for usage and display at the Bloomberg Arcade in London, hosted on the 17th/18th November 2021.

Overslept


Overslept is a VR classic escape room, where you, the hapless scientist, have overslept and missed your ride to Mars. Thankfully, with the help of the facilities friendly AI, you have the opportunity to reach the last space shuttle and make it to the barren orange space rock that is Mars.
